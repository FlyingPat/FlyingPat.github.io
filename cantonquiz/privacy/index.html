Privacy Policy

Kanton-Quiz  ·  Last updated: October 2026

Kanton-Quiz is developed and published by Patrick Hardie ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how.

Data I collect

I collect no personal data. No account is needed to play. Your scores, achievements and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to me.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score, the text goes to the app you pick through the standard share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Advertising

The current version of the app shows no ads. The Google Mobile Ads software included in the iPhone and iPad versions may still contact Google's servers when the app starts, as described in how Google uses data from partner apps. If ads are shown in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The Mac version contains no advertising software.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. Apple may collect diagnostic data if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children and has no features that ask for it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
